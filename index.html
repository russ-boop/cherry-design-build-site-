Why Bristol homeowners and investors use us

We started on the tools as bricklayers, plasterers and roofers, and have been running design and build projects in Bristol since 2016. Today we act as Principal Designer and Principal Contractor, which means one team is responsible for your drawings, approvals, safety and the build itself.

Every site is run by an SMSTS-trained manager, every job is priced line by line, and you get one point of contact from the first visit to the building control completion certificate.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover all of Bristol?

Yes. We work across Bristol and South Gloucestershire, including Henleaze, Westbury-on-Trym, Horfield, Bishopston, Lockleaze, Southmead, Brentry, Filton, Fishponds, Knowle and Bedminster.

Is the online price a quote?

No. The calculator gives a budget estimate so you know roughly where you stand. We confirm a fixed written quote after a free site visit and once the design is agreed.

Do you do the drawings or do I need an architect?

We run design and build. We prepare or coordinate the drawings and structural calculations, take them through planning and building regulations, then build the project.
