What a full refurbishment covers

A full refurbishment brings an older or tired house up to modern standards in one go. That can include removing walls to open up the layout, a full rewire and new consumer unit, new plumbing and heating, insulation, new windows, plastering, kitchens, bathrooms and decoration.

Doing it as one project is quicker and usually cheaper than piecemeal work, because each trade is sequenced properly and nothing is ripped out twice.

Structural changes

Knocking through a load-bearing wall needs a steel beam designed by a structural engineer and approved by building control. We arrange the design, the temporary support and the sign-off.

Combined with an extension or loft

Many of our refurbishments run alongside an extension or loft conversion, so the new and existing parts of the house are finished to the same standard.

Frequently asked questions

Can I live in the house during a full refurbishment?

Sometimes, room by room, but a full refurbishment is faster and safer with the house empty. We plan the phasing with you.

Do I need building regulations for a refurbishment?

Structural changes, rewiring, new heating and new windows all fall under building regulations. We arrange the approvals and certificates.
