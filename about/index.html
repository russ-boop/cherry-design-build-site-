Our story

Cherry Design & Build was founded by Russ Khalaji, who started out in Bristol as a bricklayer, plasterer and roofer cutting roofs by hand. After 18 years in construction, including trading in Bristol since 2016 as Cherry CPS, the business now designs and builds extensions, loft conversions, refurbishments and investment projects across the city.

Same people, same standards, and a name that says what we do: design and build.

How we work

  • One point of contact from the first visit to the completion certificate
  • Principal Designer and Principal Contractor under CDM 2015, so safety and design are properly managed
  • SMSTS-trained site management with a written programme for every job
  • Itemised pricing so you can see where your money goes

Qualifications

SMSTS site management, CSCS Manager card and first aid trained, with a Level 7 NVQ in Construction Senior Management in progress.
