Frequently asked questions

Do I need planning permission to convert a house to an HMO in Bristol?

A large HMO (seven or more people) always needs planning permission. A small HMO (three to six people) can often be created without planning, except in Bristol's Article 4 areas, where planning is required.

Do HMOs need a licence in Bristol?

HMOs with five or more people from two or more households need a mandatory licence, and Bristol City Council also runs additional licensing schemes in some areas. We design to the council's standards.

What profit margin do development lenders want?

Many development lenders look for a profit of 15 to 20% of the sale value (GDV). The calculator shows this figure for your deal.
