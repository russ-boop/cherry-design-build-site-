Built like a building, not a shed

We build garden rooms on proper foundations with an insulated floor, walls and roof, so they stay warm in winter and cool in summer. Each one comes with power, lighting and data, and heating if you need it, so you can use it every day of the year.

What people use them for

  • Home offices away from the noise of the house
  • Home gyms with a strong floor for weights
  • Studios, music rooms and hobby rooms
  • Guest rooms and annexes (these usually need planning)

Planning rules for garden rooms

Most garden rooms are permitted development if they are single storey, not in front of the house, cover no more than half the garden, and are no more than 2.5 m high when within 2 m of a boundary. Rooms used for sleeping, and garden rooms in conservation areas or at listed buildings, usually need planning permission.

Building regulations depend on size and use. Small outbuildings without sleeping accommodation are often exempt, but larger rooms and anything with a bathroom or bedroom need approval. We confirm what applies to yours.

Pricing

Garden rooms are priced on size, finish, glazing, access to the garden and the services you need. Call us or send us your details for a free visit and fixed quote.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need planning permission for a garden room?

Usually not, if it is single storey, behind the house, covers less than half the garden and is under 2.5 m high near a boundary. Sleeping use or a conservation area changes that.

Can a garden room have a toilet or shower?

Yes, with a drainage connection. It will need building regulations approval and we plan the drainage route at survey.

How long does a garden room take?

Most garden rooms take 4 to 8 weeks on site depending on size and finish.
